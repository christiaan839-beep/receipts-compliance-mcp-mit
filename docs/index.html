Receipts Compliance for Claude

A Claude Desktop Extension that runs on your machine. It checks a signed receipt bundle against an Ed25519 public key, then builds auditor-ready reports from your receipts: EU AI Act Annex IV, ISO/IEC 42001 and 23894, NIST AI RMF, SOC 2, GDPR DPIA, HIPAA and the EU Cyber Resilience Act.

Nothing is uploaded. The extension makes no network requests.
